Yoga Accessible to All

Online Group Yoga is designed to make yoga available to everyone.

Students can join the classes without compulsory fee pressure. After the trial, students may continue with voluntary contribution. There is no minimum or suggested donation amount.

A portion of contributions supports charity, yoga promotion, wellness education and community outreach.

A simple, consistent and supportive way to build health, calmness and discipline from home.

Beginner Friendly

Who Can Join?

Online Group Yoga is suitable for beginners, students, working professionals, homemakers and senior learners who can follow general yoga practice.

Students with specific medical conditions, injuries or special requirements should inform us before joining so we can guide them properly.
