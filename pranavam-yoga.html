Yoga Accessible to All

We believe yoga should be available to everyone regardless of financial circumstances.

Our online yoga programs operate on a donation basis. Students may contribute any amount they feel comfortable with, and those unable to contribute are still welcome to participate.

A portion of all contributions is dedicated to:

  • Supporting charitable initiatives
  • Promoting yoga education
  • Expanding access to wellness programs
  • Community outreach projects
