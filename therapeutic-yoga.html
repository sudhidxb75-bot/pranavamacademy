Benefits of Therapeutic Yoga

A calmer and more personal yoga approach for students who need gentle guidance and focused support.

Personalized Practice

Sessions can be guided according to age, flexibility, lifestyle, comfort level and wellness goals.

Stress Relief

Breathing, relaxation and mindful movement help reduce tension and support emotional balance.

Gentle Mobility

Simple movements support flexibility, joint mobility and body awareness without forceful practice.

Posture Awareness

Helpful for working professionals and students who spend long hours sitting or using devices.

Breathing Support

Pranayama-based practices support calmness, focus and better daily energy.

Progress Guidance

Regular review helps adjust the practice as the student improves gradually.

Suitable For

  • Beginners who prefer gentle one-to-one guidance
  • Students looking for stress relief and relaxation
  • Working professionals needing posture and breathing support
  • Senior learners who need a slower yoga approach
  • Students with specific wellness goals who need personalized attention

Important Note

Therapeutic Yoga at Pranavam Academy is a wellness-support program.

It is not a substitute for medical treatment, diagnosis or physiotherapy. Students with health conditions, injuries, pregnancy-related concerns or doctor-advised restrictions should consult their healthcare professional and inform us before joining.
