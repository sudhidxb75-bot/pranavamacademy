What is Therapeutic Yoga?

Therapeutic Yoga is a personalized approach that uses yoga postures, breathing techniques, relaxation methods and mindfulness practices to support physical, mental and emotional wellbeing.

Every session is adapted to your needs, comfort level and wellness goals. The focus is gentle, safe, supportive and practical for daily life.

Conditions We Commonly Support

Therapeutic yoga can support better movement, relaxation, breathing and lifestyle balance.

Physical Wellbeing

Mobility & Body Support

Back pain support, neck and shoulder stiffness, joint stiffness, posture improvement, arthritis support and mobility enhancement.

Stress Relief

Mind & Lifestyle Balance

Stress management, anxiety support, better sleep, fatigue management, workplace stress relief and emotional balance.

General Wellness

Daily Wellness Support

Improved flexibility, better breathing, increased energy, healthy ageing, relaxation and preventive wellness practices.

Benefits of Therapeutic Yoga

Gentle yoga practices can help you feel more balanced, relaxed and energetic.

  • Improved flexibility and mobility
  • Reduced stress and better relaxation
  • Better sleep quality
  • Improved posture and body awareness
  • Enhanced breathing and energy levels
  • Greater mental clarity and emotional balance

Who Can Join?

No prior yoga experience is required.

  • Beginners
  • Working professionals
  • Senior citizens
  • People seeking better flexibility and relaxation
  • Individuals looking for stress relief and lifestyle balance

Important Note

Therapeutic Yoga is designed to support overall wellbeing and healthy lifestyle practices. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or replace professional medical care. Participants with medical conditions should consult their healthcare provider before beginning any new exercise program.
